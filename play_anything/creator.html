    The monthly picture
    How these figures are calculated
    Gross revenue = paying customers × price. Contribution = gross − refunds − payment/platform fees − variable module costs − AI usage. Operating result = contribution − fixed module costs − shared hosting − maintenance labor − overhead − acquisition spend. Hosting usage is held at the entered scenario when computing break-even; future tier changes require recalculation. Break-even keeps your paid-to-active ratio constant. Setup payback uses a stable positive monthly operating result. Figures exclude tax, financing, depreciation, and engine royalties unless you enter them into the relevant allowances.
